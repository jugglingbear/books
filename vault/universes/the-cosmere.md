The Cosmere brings together Brandon Sanderson's stories across a shared universe of strikingly different worlds.
Each has its own cultures, conflicts, and expressions of magic, from Mistborn's metal-powered societies to the
storm-shaped landscapes of The Stormlight Archive. Their adventures stand within a larger setting whose connections
unfold gradually, inviting readers to explore individual series and smaller tales while discovering a wider horizon.
